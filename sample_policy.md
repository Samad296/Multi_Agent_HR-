# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

Note: this is synthetic sample data for testing. It does not describe a real company.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and unused days up to a maximum of 5 may be carried over into the next year. Requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance through the HR system and are approved by the employee's manager. Public holidays are not counted against annual leave.

## Sick Leave

Every employee receives 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive days. Employees must inform their manager before the start of the working day when they are unwell. Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out.

## Remote Work

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval and the needs of the team. Remote workers must be available during core hours from 10:00 to 16:00 and must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Remote work is not available during the probation period.

## Working Hours

The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday, from 9:00 to 17:00 with a one hour lunch break. Overtime must be approved in advance by a manager. Approved overtime is compensated with time off in lieu at the same rate, to be taken within 30 days.

## Probation Period

New employees complete a probation period of 3 months. Performance is reviewed at the end of the first and third month. During probation, either side may end the employment with 7 days written notice. Annual leave is available during probation but must be approved by the manager.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses are reimbursed when they are pre-approved and supported by receipts. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense through the finance portal. Travel must be booked through the approved travel provider. Alcohol, personal entertainment, and fines are not reimbursable.

## Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are not tolerated and may result in disciplinary action up to termination. Confidential company and client information must not be shared outside the company. Concerns can be raised confidentially with HR.

## Resignation and Notice Period

Employees who have completed probation must give 30 days written notice of resignation. The company may ask the employee to work through the notice period or place them on garden leave. Final pay, including any accrued unused annual leave, is processed within 14 days of the last working day. Company equipment must be returned on the final day.
