# Northwind Tech Employee Policy

Note: this is synthetic sample data for testing. It does not describe a real company.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to 10 unused days may be carried over to the next year, and any days beyond that expire on 31 March. Leave requests need at least 14 days notice for periods longer than 5 days, and are approved by the department head.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 8 days of paid sick leave each year. A doctor's note is required from the first day when sick leave is taken next to a public holiday, and after 3 consecutive days in all other cases. Employees should message their team lead before 9:30 on the day of absence.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Leave can be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months. Employees should inform HR at least 8 weeks before the expected start date.

## Hybrid Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams agree on two anchor days when everyone works from the office. Employees who work remotely must be reachable on chat between 9:30 and 15:30 and may not work from outside the country for more than 20 days per year.

## Working Hours

The standard working week is 37.5 hours, with flexible start times between 7:30 and 10:00. Overtime is paid at 1.5 times the normal rate when approved by a manager in advance. Weekend work needs written approval from the department head.

## Learning Budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 800 dollars for courses, books, and conferences. Purchases must be related to the employee's role and are reimbursed after submitting the receipt within 45 days. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.

## Equipment and Security

The company provides a laptop and a phone stipend of 30 dollars per month. Laptops must use full disk encryption and a screen lock of 5 minutes or less. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the IT desk within 24 hours. Company data must not be stored on personal cloud accounts.

## Notice Period

Employees who resign must give 60 days written notice after the probation period. Employees in the 6 month probation period give 14 days notice. Final pay, including unused leave days up to the carry-over limit, is paid with the next regular payroll after the last working day.
